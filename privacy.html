Privacy notice

Last updated: 27 October 2026

This notice explains how Krypton IELTS Practice handles information when students use the free practice app. The service is an independent preparation resource and is not affiliated with IELTS, Cambridge, the British Council, or IDP.

Information used by the app

  • Account details: your email address and, when supplied by Google, your account name and basic profile information. The app uses these details to sign you in; it does not request access to your Gmail messages.
  • Study plan: your name, IELTS test type, target band, daily study time, and optional planned test date.
  • Practice history: the module and activity you completed, your answers or response text, scores, time spent, mock test identifier, and any AI feedback you requested.
  • Optional speaking audio: if you choose to record and request AI feedback, the recording is sent for transcription. The raw recording is not saved in your practice history; the transcript and feedback may be saved with that practice session.

How information is used and stored

Krypton uses this information to create and secure your student account, show your study plan, save your work, and display your practice history. Account and practice data are stored using Supabase authentication and database services. Database policies restrict each student account to its own profile and practice records. Service administrators may access information when necessary to operate or support the app.

The app does not sell student account or practice information. It uses browser storage to maintain your sign-in session and remember your selected IELTS pathway.

Optional AI feedback

If you ask for AI feedback on a Writing or Speaking response, the task prompt and response text are sent to OpenAI to generate feedback. If you choose speaking audio transcription, the recording is also sent to OpenAI for transcription. These features are optional. AI results are practice estimates, not official IELTS scores. Review the OpenAI privacy policy and Supabase privacy notice for information about those providers.

Retention and account requests

Your profile and saved practice history remain associated with your account while it is active. If you want your account and associated records deleted, contact Krypton using the address below. Deleting an account removes its linked profile and practice records from the app database.

Contact

For questions about this notice or a request about your account data, contact careerkrypton@gmail.com.
